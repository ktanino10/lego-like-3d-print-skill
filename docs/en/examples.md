## Octoprints brick-kit archive

[Repository](https://github.com/ktanino10/octoprints-brick-kit-downloads) ·
[Japanese site](https://ktanino10.github.io/octoprints-brick-kit-downloads/ja/) ·
[English site](https://ktanino10.github.io/octoprints-brick-kit-downloads/en/) ·
[Archived r3 release](https://github.com/ktanino10/octoprints-brick-kit-downloads/releases/tag/archive-2026-09-20-r3)

A public archive of brick-style Mona / Copilot / Ducky designs, prototypes and
improvements. **Reference revision `r3-8mm-20260920` uses 8 mm common blocks.**
Keep it separate from the old 4 mm fine-detail trials and their small parts,
tiny pins and blocked-hole lessons. Physical r3 fit, retention and full assembly
remain unverified; geometry 3MF is **NOT_SLICED**. This is a separate design
family from B; do not combine their counts or physical results.

**Fixed reference versus current progress:** The public README also contains
later part-count/support-free experiments and an ongoing Copilot symmetry
revision. This is not a claim that r3 is the whole current collection, or that
every design is complete and physically validated. Check the linked README for
the latest revision-specific status and holds.

## Carry forward the workflow and the evidence discipline

Prefer reusable parts, render from manufactured geometry, connect print slots
to assembly locations and start with small trials. Do not default to copying
the original characters, colors, logos or personal inscriptions.
Old 4 mm failures are not measurements of 8 mm B. Keep design checks, reports,
photographs and physical measurements distinct.
