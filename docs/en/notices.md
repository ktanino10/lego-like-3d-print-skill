<a id="physical-safety"></a>
## Separate digital checks from physical tests

The 8 mm dimensions are independent prototype candidates, not guarantees of
commercial-block compatibility, toy certification, retention, strength, tipping
safety or life span. Keep small parts and broken sharp pieces away from children
and pets. Nominal clearance, CAD checks, finished renders and application launch
success are not physical acceptance. Start with small trials and stop for
blockage, strong force, whitening, cracks or poor seating.

Geometry 3MF is **NOT_SLICED**. Verify printer, nozzle, material, plate and profile
and inspect actual layer previews. It is not a configured P1S project, pause or
G-code. This skill does not Send/Print to a printer. B's reported physical build
covers base/front, not all 150 parts, the face or measured physical performance.

<a id="privacy-tools"></a>
## Before execution or publication

Read the skill before installing; protect existing files and unsaved documents/
scenes. MCP is optional, not installed or started automatically. Remove personal
paths from shared logs. Inspect photographs, personal inscriptions, generated
content, ZIPs and metadata, and publish only to the destination and within the
scope actually approved for the current work.

Blender cross-version compatibility is not guaranteed. In particular, a 5.1.1
`.blend` may not reproduce identically in 4.5 LTS for Intel Macs. Finished scenes
are not bundled; normally create scenes from real meshes in your supported version.

Reviewing official OS instructions, executing a local smoke check and physically
testing a model are distinct. State unrun, failed and uncertain work; static checks
or CI success alone are not proof of printing or live publication.
